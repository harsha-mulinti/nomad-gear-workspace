Manufacturer confirmation checklist

  • Confirm exact upper, lining, insole and outsole materials
  • Confirm existing molds, sizes and color options
  • Confirm branding placement and customization
  • Obtain sample quotation, quantity and lead time
  • Confirm MOQ, packaging and applicable compliance

Sample evaluation checklist

  • Record sample order and spending approval status
  • Receive and photograph actual samples
  • Test fit, comfort, grip and construction quality
  • Run two-week wear trial with three testers per model
  • Conduct badminton-specific court movement tests
  • Record approve / revise / reject decision

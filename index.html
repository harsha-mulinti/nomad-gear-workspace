05 / CUSTOMER DEVELOPMENT

Go-to-Market & Outreach.

Different customers, relevant communities, one shared commitment to introducing NOMAD footwear.

We will start with trusted introductions, sample trials and customer feedback, then expand into local sales channels as the products are validated. NG 01 and NG 02 share overlapping lifestyle channels, while the remaining categories need more targeted outreach.

NG 01 + NG 02

Casual & Lifestyle

Students, college-goers, working professionals and everyday lifestyle buyers

College and office networks, local retailers, social media, friends, family and personal referrals.

First step

Show concepts and samples, gather style and fit feedback, identify interested retail and community contacts.

NG 03

Walking & Comfort

Middle-aged and older adults seeking comfortable everyday walking shoes

Apartment communities, resident associations, senior walking groups, family connections and neighborhood introductions.

First step

Arrange community introductions and wear trials, listen to comfort and fit feedback, build trusted referrals.

NG 04

Sports & Running

Fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers and recreational runners

Gyms, trainers, fitness communities, running groups and local sports events.

First step

Seek trial feedback on cushioning, fit and durability from active users and fitness contacts.

Badminton players, coaches, clubs and academies

NOMAD Sports, coaches, badminton academies, clubs, tournaments and player networks.

Coordinate on-court sample trials and collect feedback on grip, stability, movement and wear.

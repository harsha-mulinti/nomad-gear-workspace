Design principles

  • One distinctive silhouette per category.
  • Use existing factory molds wherever practical.
  • Keep branding and colorways restrained.
  • Prioritize comfort, durability and grip over decorative details.

Key separation

  • Everyday vs Premium: simple canvas low-top versus suede-led retro sneaker.
  • Walking vs Sports: flexibility and comfort versus secure fit and training support.
  • Badminton: dedicated non-marking traction and lateral stability.

Decision gates

Before ordering samples
  • Confirm materials, construction and available molds.
  • Get per-model sample pricing, MOQ and lead times.
  • Confirm branding options and applicable certification.
Before approving production
  • Review wear-test scores and category-specific performance.
  • Calculate true landed cost and retailer margin.
  • Verify BIS, labeling and trademark requirements.
